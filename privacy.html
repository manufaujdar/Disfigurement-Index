Governance

Data and clinical governance

A simple operating framework for responsible clinical use, records, review, and updates.

Doctor-led use

The index supports clinical documentation. Assessment and interpretation remain with the treating doctor.

Case records

Each saved case keeps the score, selected domains, setting, region, and clinical notes together.

Clinical review

Doctors can share feedback to improve wording, workflow, scoring clarity, and reporting needs.

Updates

Formula changes, wording changes, and workflow changes should be reviewed before release.

Privacy

Use concise case identifiers and avoid unnecessary personal details in notes or comments.

Reports

Printed and copied summaries should be reviewed by the clinician before they are used elsewhere.

Review cycle

How the site should be maintained

  1. Review calculator domains and wording with clinicians.
  2. Check saved case summaries for clarity.
  3. Update author, guide, and governance content when project details change.
  4. Keep doctor feedback visible to the project team.
