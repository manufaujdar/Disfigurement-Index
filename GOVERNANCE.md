# Governance

Disfigurement Index is an early-stage open-source clinical research prototype.

## Maintainer

The repository maintainer is Manu Faujdar. The maintainer reviews pull
requests, releases, dependency/provenance changes, safety language, formula
changes, and future AI-image-analysis boundaries. A maintainer may reject a
change that increases clinical risk, privacy risk, licensing uncertainty, or
measurement overclaiming.

## Contributions

Contributions should include tests, documentation, provenance, and a clear
statement of what remains unvalidated. Changes involving patient data, clinical
claims, regulated intended use, AI models, datasets, or deployment require
explicit review before merge.

## Clinical and Research Decisions

This repository does not provide clinical governance. Any study or deployment
must have its own responsible clinical investigator, privacy/security owner,
validation plan, institutional approvals, and intended-use review.
Open-source maintainership is not clinical sign-off.

## Releases

Releases should update `CHANGELOG.md`, run the full test workflow, confirm
license/provenance boundaries, and state unresolved limitations. A version tag
does not imply clinical validation, regulatory clearance, or suitability for
protected health information.
