Capabilities

Designed for clinical image review

Photo quality check

Assess lighting, focus, angle, scale marker visibility, and image completeness before scoring.

Region mapping

Identify the anatomical area and outline visible zones that may need structured review.

Feature support

Assist review of color difference, contour change, surface texture, and estimated extent.

Follow-up comparison

Compare repeat images from the same case when capture conditions are suitable.

Report draft

Prepare a concise image-analysis note for clinician review before saving or printing.

Doctor review

Keep the final interpretation, score, and clinical decision under clinician control.

Planned workflow

How it will fit into the index

  1. Upload or capture a standardized clinical image.
  2. Confirm region, scale, orientation, and image quality.
  3. Review AI-assisted observations alongside manual domain scoring.
  4. Save the clinician-approved summary with the index calculation.
