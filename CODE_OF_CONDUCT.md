# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive. Do not
share patient information, private research material, clinical photographs,
credentials, or proprietary content in issues, pull requests, discussions, or
patches.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and unsafe
clinical claims are not acceptable. The repository maintainer may remove
content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors,
patients, research participants, or the project.

For sensitive concerns, use the private reporting route described in
`SECURITY.md`.
